How the ranking works

On-topic papers: papers matching your topic in the chosen window. Senior-author share: how often they are the last author, the usual position for a lab head. First authors (lab size): distinct first authors on their senior-author papers, a rough guide to lab size. Badges flag likely PIs, rising PIs (often keen to recruit) and profiles that look like students or postdocs.

Data comes from OpenAlex (250M+ scholarly records) and can be incomplete or out of date. Always confirm on the professor's own page before emailing.

In fields that list authors alphabetically (much of mathematics, theoretical CS and economics), senior-author share and lab size say little; rely on paper count and recency there.
