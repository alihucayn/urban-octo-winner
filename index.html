How the ranking works

On-topic papers: papers matching your topic in the chosen window. Senior-author share: how often they are the last author, the usual position for a lab head. Lead students: distinct first authors on their senior-author papers, a proxy for lab size. Badges flag likely PIs, rising PIs (often keen to recruit) and profiles that look like students or postdocs.

Data from OpenAlex can be incomplete or out of date. Always confirm on the professor's own page before emailing.
